# Project charter

GenAIOpenGateway is open source in full, now and later.

1. **One edition.** There is no enterprise, pro or cloud-only edition. Every feature, including SSO, SCIM, RBAC, audit logging, guardrails and multi-tenancy, lives in this repository under Apache-2.0.
2. **No license keys.** The software never checks for a license, a seat count or a paid entitlement.
3. **No relicensing.** Contributions are accepted under Apache-2.0 with a Developer Certificate of Origin sign-off. There is no CLA, so no single party can relicense the code.
4. **Open roadmap.** Plans and design decisions are made in public issues and RFCs.
5. **Neutral home.** Once three or more organizations maintain the project, it will be proposed to a neutral foundation, which will hold the name and trademark.

Companies may sell hosting, support, training or certification. They may not ask the project to withhold features to protect that business.
